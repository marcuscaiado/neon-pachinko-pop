# 🔮 Neon Pachinko Pop

[![Play Game](https://img.shields.io/badge/PLAY_NOW-FF9F43?style=for-the-badge&logo=gamepad&logoColor=black)](https://marcuscaiado.github.io/neon-pachinko-pop/)
[![Arcade Hub](https://img.shields.io/badge/🕹️_Arcade_Hub-FF6B9D?style=for-the-badge)](https://marcuscaiado.github.io/marcus-arcade/)

Satisfying neon Peggle/Pachinko bouncy drop web game with glowing multi-pegs, bumper cushions, moving jackpot cups, and soothing chimes.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay & Features

* **Elastic Bounce Physics:** Realistic momentum, gravitational acceleration, and wall ricochets.
* **4 Distinct Peg Classes:**
  * 🔵 **Blue Neon Pegs:** Base points (50 pts) + harmonic chimes.
  * 🟠 **Orange Star Pegs:** Bonus multipliers (250 pts).
  * 🟢 **Green Launcher Pegs:** Super-elastic high-velocity ricochet pads.
  * 🟣 **Magenta Multi-Ball Pegs:** Splits your sphere into multi-ball cascades.
* **Moving Jackpot Collector:** Catch dropping orbs in the bottom sliding basket to earn **FREE EXTRA ORBS** + Jackpot fanfare!
* **Mobile & Galaxy A55 Ready:** Responsive canvas scaling with safe HUD boundaries and trajectory laser dots.

---

## 🕹️ Controls

* **Aim Cannon:** Move mouse or touch & drag on screen.
* **Launch Orb:** Click / Tap screen.
* **Arcade Hub Link:** Top-right corner button.
